💡 The Challenge: Budget Constraints vs. Optical Physics

The core design of Itachi's Sharingan demands a striking red background with black accents. However, when sending the Gerber files for manufacturing, the extra fee for a custom red solder mask was outside the prototyping budget.
🛑 The Workaround: Light Diffusion

Instead of compromising the design or overspending, I opted for a standard white solder mask and used light diffusion to my advantage.

When the circuit is powered on in low-light environments, the white fiberglass acts as a natural reflector. The intense red light from the LEDs bounces across the surface, visually transforming the entire body of the earring into a vivid, glowing red. The desired anime aesthetic was achieved perfectly at night while keeping production costs low.
🔬 Hardware Architecture & Micro-Soldering

This piece relies entirely on Surface-Mount Technology (SMT), requiring extreme precision on the workbench. Everything was 100% hand-soldered.

Illumination: 4x Micro Red LEDs (0603 package, operating at 2V). Three are positioned on the outer edges to create the Sharingan spin effect, with one in the optical center.

Current Control: 4x 2.2k ohm SMD resistors (0603 package) to safely limit the current and maximize battery life.

Power Source: Powered by a standard CR2032 coin cell battery.

Switch: Built-in micro slide-switch for on-the-go activation.

💎 Industrial Design & Wearability

Like all Noberto wearables, the technological function goes hand-in-hand with premium fashion aesthetics:

Exposed Copper Logo: I manipulated the solder mask layers in the PCB design software to leave the copper layer completely exposed for the "Noberto" logo. This detail gives the earring a raw, heavy, and industrial cyberpunk look.

Premium Battery Holder: Unlike standard plastic mounts, I adapted a sleek metal holder for the battery, perfectly matching the silver tones of the overall piece.

Comfort & Safety: The hardware is mounted to a high-quality, silver-plated earring hook, ensuring the piece is hypoallergenic and comfortable for long hours of cosplay or daily wear.
